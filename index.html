How this tool works
1Describe the project
Name it, set how long it runs and how many sites it covers. Duration can be entered directly in months or derived from a start and end date.
2Allocate the sites
Split the total across products and connectivity modes (WAN) or device tiers (LAN). The allocation must add up to the total before the estimate will run.
3Calculate
Every calculation is saved automatically and shows its full working, so any number can be traced back to the rate card.
📖 Unsure what a term means or where a rate comes from? Rates & Method in the sidebar lists every published rate, the effort formula and a glossary.

How an estimate is built
WAN — base effort by product and connectivity mode

Figures are man-days of DPM effort for a single site, before any complexity multiplier. A dash means that product is not offered with that connectivity mode — it does not mean zero effort.

— combination not offered MD man-day: one person, one full day CPE customer premises equipment
LAN — base effort by device tier

A site's tier is decided by how many network devices it contains. In Standard mode the base effort below is used directly; in By Stage mode it is replaced by the stage hours you select.

LAN — stage effort detail

Figures are hours per site for each delivery activity. In By Stage mode the hours of every activity in the selected stages are added together and divided by 8 to produce man-days per site.
